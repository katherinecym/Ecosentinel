DO mg/L i
💧 Dissolved Oxygen (DO) In-situ optical luminescent sensor measurement. Levels below 4.0 mg/L represent hypoxic stagnant waters, which trigger accelerated Culex oviposition and larval survival.

Velocity i
🌊 Stream Surface Velocity (m/s) Telemetry radar velocity reading. Flow speeds above 0.30 m/s induce critical hydraulic shear that dislodges egg rafts and prevents larval anchoring. 0.30 m/s is a pre-specified operational marker: the threshold-shape test is null (p = 0.84), so treat the cutoff as a design assumption, not an established tipping point.
