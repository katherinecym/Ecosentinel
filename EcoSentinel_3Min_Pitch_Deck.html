Cities spray millions of liters of chemical biocides to kill mosquitoes, yet West Nile Virus outbreaks keep rising. Why? Because healthy flowing rivers don't breed mosquitoes.

⚠️ The "Urban Stream Syndrome" Paradox

Urban concrete and organic pollution create stagnant hypoxia (DO runs 2.1–7.2 mg/L across our 85 reaches; mean 4.8 mg/L). This suffocates insect-eating fish and dragonflies, leaving the hyper-tolerant Culex pipiens vector free from all predators. Chemical fogging only kills the remaining fish—worsening the cycle.
